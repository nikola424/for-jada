01

Why you’re here, and why it isn’t enough

You’re here right now because you came across my page. The scholarship lists, the deadlines, the ones that don’t even ask for a 4.0.

But a list can only get you so far. If you save every post but never send an application, nothing changes. If your child writes the same essay as everyone else, it gets lost in a pile of thousands.

02

The promise, and the first proof

I won over six hundred thousand dollars in scholarships myself. And now I help students do the same.

So if you stick with me through this short video, I’m going to show you how your child can pay for college without student loans, even if they don’t have a perfect GPA.

One of my students told me after our first session: “Ms Foote was very helpful when it came to listing out different ways I could earn money for college, many of them I had never heard of before.” Another wrote to me that she won a scholarship she wouldn’t have known about otherwise.

03

What this isn’t

But right off the bat, I want you to know this isn’t about applying to five hundred scholarships and hoping one sticks. It’s not about paying an essay mill. And it’s not about being the valedictorian.

04

Why now

You’re watching this at a good time. Scholarships have deadlines all year, and every month you wait, a few of them close for good. Senior year moves fast. The families who start early are the ones who don’t end up signing loan papers in August.

05

Who this is for

This is for high school seniors, college students and their parents who want to pay for school without loans. If your child has good grades but not perfect ones. If you’ve looked at scholarship sites and felt lost. If you want someone who’s actually won this money to show you how.

06

The three things I’ll prove

In the next few minutes I’m going to prove three things to you. One, why applying only to the big scholarships is a costly mistake. Two, where the money most students miss is hiding. And three, why you don’t need a 4.0 to win it.

07

The costly mistake

Most students apply to the big national scholarships. The ten-thousand-dollar ones everybody sees. So thousands of students apply, and almost all of them hear nothing back.

Meanwhile there are local, niche and HBCU scholarships that barely anyone applies to. Some ask for a 2.5 GPA. Some don’t ask for a GPA at all. Those are the ones that add up.

08

How it works, through one student

Let me walk you through what this looks like. In our first session we list every kind of scholarship your child qualifies for, many of them ones you’ve never heard of.

Then we build one strong story your child can use across their essays, and change it a little for each one. We plan who to ask for recommendations and when. And every month we look at what’s due next, so nothing slips past.

One student told me our meeting was a ten out of ten, because I answered all her questions about scholarships, essays and recommendations in one place.

09

What I learned the hard way

When I was applying, nobody gave me a plan. I found scholarships late, I missed deadlines, and I wrote some essays I’m not proud of. What changed everything was treating it like a system, not a lottery. That’s exactly what I teach now.

10

What you get when you work with me

When you work with me, you get one-on-one scholarship coaching, a list of scholarships matched to your child, help with every essay and recommendation, and a plan for each month’s deadlines. Your child does the applying, and I make sure every application counts.

11

The students, and the next step

Students who found my page have already won scholarships they would never have found on their own.

If you want that for your child, tap the button below and see if you qualify. You don’t need a perfect GPA. You need a plan, and I’ll build it with you.
